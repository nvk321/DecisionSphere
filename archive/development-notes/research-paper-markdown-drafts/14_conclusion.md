### 13.1 Summary of Contributions
In this paper, we presented **DecisionSphere**, a cross-domain Decision Intelligence platform that addresses the pervasive problem of organizational analytical silos in enterprise strategic planning. By bridging statistical machine learning predictions with deterministic managerial accounting and evidence-grounded recommendation rules, DecisionSphere enables organizational decision-makers to evaluate the systemic ripple effects of commercial shocks before commitment.

Our core findings and accomplishments include:
1. **Resolution of the Margin Erosion Paradox:** We demonstrated mathematically and empirically that an apparently successful promotional sales campaign ($+15\%$ volume) can destroy enterprise operating profit ($-\$380,453$ net loss) due to downstream delivery penalties and workforce turnover replacement drag ($C_{\text{replace}} = \$30,000$ per departed worker).
2. **Probabilistic Calibration Efficacy:** We proved that post-hoc Sigmoid (Platt) scaling significantly improves probability calibration (reducing Brier scores by $12.5\%$ in logistics and $17.9\%$ in workforce retention), providing reliable probabilities for financial currency multiplication.
3. **The 6-Tier Epistemic Hierarchy:** We formalized a classification taxonomy (`OBSERVED`, `PREDICTED`, `ASSUMED`, `PROPAGATED`, `CALCULATED`, `RECOMMENDED`) that enforces the critical scientific distinction between predictive association and physical causality.
4. **End-to-End System Prototyping (TRL 4):** We validated a full-stack, laboratory-verified architecture comprising frozen XGBoost models, a FastAPI backend orchestrator, a Next.js interactive decision interface, and an executable 16-part Jupyter research suite.

### 13.3 Future Research Trajectory (Phase 2 Roadmap)
Building upon the completed TRL 4 foundation, future extensions will focus on advancing toward TRL 6 (field validation):
1. **Structural Causal Modeling (SCM):** Implementing do-calculus and instrumental variable estimation to transition from conditional predictive associations to formal causal counterfactual inference.
2. **Multi-Objective Pareto Optimization:** Augmenting the deterministic rule engine with multi-objective genetic algorithms (e.g., NSGA-II) to compute non-dominated Pareto frontiers balancing profit maximization against workforce burnout.
3. **Federated Enterprise Ingestion:** Extending the data ingestion pipeline to support secure, federated queries across enterprise ERP systems (e.g., SAP, Salesforce) without centralizing sensitive proprietary records.

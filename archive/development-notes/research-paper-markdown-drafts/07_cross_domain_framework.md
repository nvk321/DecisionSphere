### 6.1 The Operational Coupling Mechanism
The core contribution of DecisionSphere is the formalization of cross-domain operational propagation: translating a commercial shock $\theta$ applied to sales into physical operational strains across logistics dispatch and workforce schedules.

```
Commercial Lever (Promo +15%)
            │
            ▼
Retail Demand ML Regressor
            │ Projected Cycle Volume (V_scen)
            ▼
[ASM-007, ASM-008: Volume Scaling Engine]
            │ Dispatched Order Batch (Delta O)
            ▼
Logistics Order Generation & Delivery Risk Classifier
            │ Calibrated Late Probability (p_late) & Order Pressure
            ▼
[ASM-006: Operational Pressure Coupling Engine]
            │ Workload Ratio -> Overtime Headcount (W_ot)
            ▼
Workforce Feature Shift (OverTime='Yes', Delta WLB = -1.0)
            │
            ▼
Workforce Retention Classifier
            │ Calibrated Attrition Probability (p_att)
            ▼
Deterministic Financial Accounting Engine
```

### 6.2 Mathematical Translation Equations

#### Step 1: Demand to Logistics Dispatch Scaling
Let $V_{\text{base}}$ be the baseline forecasted demand over the 14-day cycle, and let $\hat{V}_{\text{scen}}$ be the simulated scenario forecast. The volume delta ratio is:
$$r_{\text{vol}} = \frac{\hat{V}_{\text{scen}} - V_{\text{base}}}{V_{\text{base}}}$$

Under `ASM-007` ($V_{\text{baseline}} = 10,000$ units) and `ASM-008` ($O_{\text{baseline}} = 1,000$ parcels), the incremental dispatched orders $\Delta O$ generated by the commercial shock are:
$$\Delta O = \text{round}\left(O_{\text{baseline}} \times r_{\text{vol}}\right)$$
$$O_{\text{scen}} = O_{\text{baseline}} + \Delta O$$

#### Step 2: Logistics Late Delivery Risk Inference
The dispatched order batch $O_{\text{scen}}$ is evaluated by the calibrated supply chain classifier under user-specified lead-time offsets ($\Delta_{\text{sla\_days}}$) and shipping mode overrides. The expected late delivery volume is:
$$O_{\text{late}} = O_{\text{scen}} \times \hat{P}(\text{Late}=1 \mid \mathbf{x}_{\text{logistics}})$$

#### Step 3: Logistics Pressure to Workforce Overtime Translation
Elevated dispatch volume and expedited delivery requirements increase operational workload pressure $P_{\text{ops}}$:
$$P_{\text{ops}} = \frac{O_{\text{scen}}}{O_{\text{baseline}}} \times \left(1 + \frac{O_{\text{late}}}{O_{\text{scen}}}\right)$$

If the user specifies an explicit overtime lever $\theta_{\text{ot}} \in [0, 100\%]$, this ratio directly sets the proportion of the workforce assigned to mandatory overtime:
$$r_{\text{ot}} = \frac{\theta_{\text{ot}}}{100}$$

Under `ASM-006`, employees shifted to mandatory overtime experience an operational fatigue penalty on their reported work-life balance:
$$\text{WLB}_{\text{scen}} = \max\left(1, \text{WLB}_{\text{base}} + \Delta \text{WLB}\right) \quad \text{where } \Delta \text{WLB} = -1.0$$

#### Step 4: Workforce Attrition Inference
The updated employee feature vectors (with `OverTime='Yes'` and $\text{WLB}_{\text{scen}}$) are processed by the calibrated workforce classifier, yielding the scenario attrition probability:
$$p_{\text{att\_scen}} = \hat{P}(\text{Attrition}=1 \mid \mathbf{x}_{\text{workforce\_scen}})$$
The incremental departure probability is $\Delta p_{\text{att}} = p_{\text{att\_scen}} - p_{\text{att\_base}}$.

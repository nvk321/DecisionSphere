### 7.1 Financial Accounting Rationale
In corporate enterprise environments, machine learning predictions must ultimately be evaluated in currency units. Rather than using black-box neural networks or language models to guess financial outcomes, DecisionSphere implements a **Deterministic Managerial Accounting Engine** based on transparent, auditable formulas grounded in managerial economics.

### 7.2 The Governing Accounting Equations

#### 7.2.1 Gross and Realized Revenue Delta
The incremental gross revenue generated by commercial volume expansion is governed by `ASM-001` (Unit Selling Price $P_{\text{unit}} = \$25.00$):
$$\Delta R_{\text{gross}} = (\hat{V}_{\text{scen}} - V_{\text{base}}) \times P_{\text{unit}}$$

Late deliveries cause customer dissatisfaction, order cancellations, and concessions. Realized revenue is adjusted by an empirical fulfillment discount:
$$\Delta R_{\text{realized}} = \Delta R_{\text{gross}} \times \left(1 - \Delta p_{\text{late}} \times r_{\text{cancel}}\right)$$
where $r_{\text{cancel}} = 0.05$ ($5\%$ cancellation rate per incremental unit of late delivery probability).

#### 7.2.2 Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Delta
Under `ASM-002`, direct production and manufacturing expenses are modeled as a fixed ratio of realized revenue ($r_{\text{COGS}} = 0.60$, representing a $40\%$ baseline gross commercial margin):
$$\Delta \text{COGS} = \Delta R_{\text{realized}} \times r_{\text{COGS}}$$

#### 7.2.3 Logistics Fulfillment & Carrier Penalty Delta
Logistics expenses encompass standard handling and packaging per parcel (`ASM-003`, $C_{\text{base}} = \$8.50$) plus contractual carrier SLA breach penalties (`ASM-005`, $C_{\text{penalty}} = \$25.00$):
$$\Delta C_{\text{logistics}} = \Delta O \times C_{\text{base}} + \left(O_{\text{scen}} \cdot p_{\text{late\_scen}} \cdot C_{\text{penalty}} - O_{\text{base}} \cdot p_{\text{late\_base}} \cdot C_{\text{penalty}}\right)$$

#### 7.2.4 Workforce Labor & Turnover Replacement Drag
Workforce expenditure comprises three distinct line items:
1. **Overtime Payroll Premium:**
   $$\Delta C_{\text{ot}} = W_{\text{headcount}} \times \Delta r_{\text{ot}} \times \overline{\text{Salary}}_{\text{monthly}} \times 0.25$$
   where $\overline{\text{Salary}}_{\text{monthly}} = \$5,000.00$ and $0.25$ represents the statutory $25\%$ overtime premium.
2. **Turnover Replacement Drag:**
   Replacing departed personnel requires advertising, agency recruitment fees, candidate interviews, onboarding training, and productivity loss. Under SHRM guidelines (`ASM-004`), replacement cost is parameterized as $C_{\text{replace}} = \$30,000.00$ per departed employee:
   $$\Delta C_{\text{turnover}} = W_{\text{headcount}} \times \Delta p_{\text{att}} \times C_{\text{replace}}$$
3. **Discretionary Compensation Adjustments:**
   $$\Delta C_{\text{hike}} = W_{\text{headcount}} \times \overline{\text{Salary}}_{\text{monthly}} \times \frac{\theta_{\text{salary\_hike}}}{100}$$
Total Workforce Cost Delta:
$$\Delta C_{\text{workforce}} = \Delta C_{\text{ot}} + \Delta C_{\text{turnover}} + \Delta C_{\text{hike}}$$

#### 7.2.5 Net Operating Margin Delta ($\Delta \Pi$)
The bottom-line enterprise impact is the net operating margin delta:
$$\Delta \Pi = \Delta R_{\text{realized}} - \left(\Delta \text{COGS} + \Delta C_{\text{logistics}} + \Delta C_{\text{workforce}}\right)$$

### 7.3 Resolving the "Growth Destroys Value" Paradox
A central finding of DecisionSphere is explaining how a successful promotional campaign can lead to negative profit:
$$\Delta R_{\text{realized}} = +\$3,856 \implies \Delta \text{Gross Margin} = +\$1,542$$
$$\Delta C_{\text{logistics}} = +\$496 \quad \text{and} \quad \Delta C_{\text{workforce}} = +\$381,500$$
$$\Delta \Pi = +\$1,542 - \$496 - \$381,500 = -\$380,454$$

Because labor replacement costs ($\$30,000$ per worker) and overtime payroll scale aggressively with employee fatigue, the cost of employee turnover easily exceeds commercial gross margin gains on marginal volume surges.

This directory contains the complete intellectual property (IP), prior art, patentability, and invention disclosure portfolio for **DecisionSphere: A Scenario-Based Strategic Planning and Risk Evaluation Platform**.

In adherence to Rule 5 of the Master Program:

- **Defensible Technical Core:** The candidate invention is strictly restricted to the **computer-implemented state-space dependency graph architecture, bounded state propagation operator, and probability-to-penalty error-bounding mechanisms** orchestrating heterogeneous predictive models across continuous state vectors in $\mathbb{R}^{24}$.

## 3. Mandatory Pre-Filing Secrecy Warning

> [!WARNING]
> Under Indian patent law (Section 29–34 of the Indian Patents Act, 1970) and international patent conventions (Paris Convention / PCT), public disclosure of enabling technical details prior to filing a Provisional or Complete Patent Application destroys absolute novelty.
> 
> Therefore:
> 1. Do NOT publish enabling mathematical formulas or serialized graph propagation logic in open conference proceedings or public repositories before the institutional IPR cell makes a formal filing decision.
> 2. The final decision on patent eligibility and application filing MUST be reviewed and approved by registered patent attorneys or the university IPR cell.

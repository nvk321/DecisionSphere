# DecisionSphere — Peer-Reviewed Publication Venue Shortlist & Submission Strategy

**Document ID:** `DSP-PUB-VENUE-001`  
**Date:** 2026-10-24  
**Authoritative Scope:** Peer-reviewed IEEE / ACM / Elsevier / Springer conferences and journals with verified Scopus / SCI-E indexing.  
**Strict Anti-Predatory Rule:** No predatory publishers, no guaranteed-acceptance venues, no unverified index claims.  

---

## 1. Publication Strategy Overview & Venue Taxonomy

The publication strategy for DecisionSphere is bifurcated into two tracks:
1. **Conference Track (6–8 pages):** Focuses on the **systems architecture, cross-domain scenario propagation framework, and preliminary empirical validation** (EXP-001 through EXP-007). Ideal for presenting the TRL 4 working prototype to the international academic community.
2. **Journal Track (14–18 pages):** Focuses on the **formal mathematical state-space formulation, complete asymptotic statistical analysis (EXP-008, EXP-009), calibration error bounding, comprehensive sensitivity analysis, and managerial decision theory implications**.

## 2. Conference Shortlist

### Tier A — Best Realistic Fit (Primary Targets)

#### 1. IEEE International Conference on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics (IEEE SMC)
- **Flagship Sponsor:** IEEE Systems, Man, and Cybernetics Society.
- **Indexing:** Scopus, Web of Science (CPCI-S), IEEE Xplore, Google Scholar.
- **Scope Alignment:** High. Specific technical committees on *Decision Intelligence*, *Human-Machine Systems*, *Enterprise Information Systems*, and *Cybernetics for Complex Systems*.
- **Submission Format:** Standard IEEE 2-column conference format.
- **Page Limit:** 6 pages standard (up to 8 pages with overlength page fee).
- **Publication Model:** Hybrid / Standard conference proceeding. No mandatory author APC for accepted papers registered by authors.
- **Review Process:** Rigorous peer review (typically 3 independent expert reviewers).
- **Deadlines:** Annual CFP typically closes in March/April; conference held in October.
- **Why It Fits DecisionSphere:** DecisionSphere's coupling of human workforce burnout, logistics cybernetic feedback, and continuous state-space propagation directly aligns with the core SMC mandate.

#### 2. IEEE International Conference on Technology and Engineering Management (IEEE TEMSCON)
- **Flagship Sponsor:** IEEE Technology and Engineering Management Society (TEMS).
- **Indexing:** Scopus, IEEE Xplore, EI Compendex.
- **Scope Alignment:** Exceptional. Focuses on bridging technical AI algorithms with strategic organizational decision-making, managerial accounting, and technology risk evaluation.
- **Submission Format:** IEEE 2-column conference format.
- **Page Limit:** 6–8 pages.
- **Publication Model:** Standard IEEE conference registration ($0 mandatory open access APC).
- **Review Process:** Single-blind peer review.
- **Deadlines:** Regional tracks throughout Q1–Q3.
- **Why It Fits DecisionSphere:** Directly addresses the project's managerial decision intelligence paradigm (capital misallocation avoidance, multi-criteria risk gating).

#### 3. ACM International Conference on Information and Knowledge Management (CIKM) — Applied Research Track
- **Flagship Sponsor:** ACM SIGIR and SIGWEB.
- **Indexing:** Scopus, Web of Science, ACM Digital Library, DBLP.
- **Scope Alignment:** High. Applied Research Track specifically seeks end-to-end data pipelines solving concrete multi-domain enterprise problems using tabular ML models and explainable AI.
- **Submission Format:** ACM Primary Article Template (single-column or double-column per ACM guidelines).
- **Page Limit:** 8 pages content + unlimited references.
- **Publication Model:** ACM Open Access model.
- **Review Process:** Double-blind peer review (reproducibility and empirical rigor heavily weighted).
- **Why It Fits DecisionSphere:** CIKM values empirical benchmarks, ablation studies, and open reproducibility packages.

## 3. Journal Shortlist

### Tier A — Best Realistic Fit (Primary Targets)

#### 1. IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management (IEEE TEM)
- **Publisher:** IEEE Technology and Engineering Management Society.
- **Indexing:** SCI-E, Scopus (Q1), Web of Science Core Collection.
- **Impact Factor:** $\approx 4.6$ (JCR 2023/2024).
- **Scope Alignment:** Ideal. Focuses on the intersection of engineering methodologies, data science, and managerial strategic planning.
- **Submission Format:** IEEE Transactions double-column (or single-column double-spaced review format).
- **Page Limit:** Typically 12–16 published pages (approximately 30–40 double-spaced manuscript pages).
- **Publication Model:** Hybrid ($0 author fee under standard subscription model; optional Gold OA APC: $2,495).
- **Review Process:** Rigorous double-blind peer review (typically 2–3 rounds).
- **Why It Fits DecisionSphere:** DecisionSphere's formalization of capital misallocation avoidance, deterministic managerial accounting waterfalls, and sensitivity analysis represents the exact core contribution targeted by IEEE TEM.

#### 2. Decision Support Systems (Elsevier)
- **Publisher:** Elsevier.
- **Indexing:** SCI-E, Scopus (Q1 in Decision Sciences & Computer Science), Web of Science.
- **Impact Factor:** $\approx 6.7$ (JCR 2023/2024).
- **Scope Alignment:** High. The premier international journal dedicated to computer-implemented systems that assist organizational decision-makers.
- **Submission Format:** Elsevier standard single-column manuscript format.
- **Length:** 8,000–10,000 words (including figures and tables).
- **Publication Model:** Transformative Hybrid Journal ($0 APC under standard subscription; optional Gold OA APC: $3,470).
- **Review Process:** Single-blind peer review.
- **Why It Fits DecisionSphere:** Explicitly seeks papers combining statistical machine learning with decision theory, multi-criteria risk scoring, and interactive scenario simulation.

#### 3. ACM Transactions on Management Information Systems (ACM TMIS)
- **Publisher:** Association for Computing Machinery (ACM).
- **Indexing:** SCI-E, Scopus (Q1), ACM Digital Library.
- **Impact Factor:** $\approx 3.5$.
- **Scope Alignment:** Excellent. Specializes in technical systems that leverage data analytics and algorithms to solve organizational management problems.
- **Length:** Up to 35 double-spaced manuscript pages.
- **Publication Model:** ACM Open Access model.
- **Review Process:** Rigorous peer review.

- **Publication Model:** Hybrid ($0 APC under subscription).
- **Assessment:** High empirical rigor required; would require emphasizing the DataCo supply chain delay classification and logistics cost functions.

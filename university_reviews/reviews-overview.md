## 3. Project Core Alignment Summary

- **Aim:** To design and develop an AI-powered strategic decision support platform that enables organizations to simulate business scenarios, evaluate cross-domain risks across demand forecasting, supply chain, and workforce management, and generate explainable recommendations for proactive strategic decision-making.
- **Sustainable Development Goal (SDG):** SDG 9 – Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure.
- **Technology Readiness Level (TRL):** Progressing from TRL 2 (Technology Concept Formulated at Review 1) to TRL 4 (Component/subsystem validation in laboratory environment at Review 2).
- **Outcomes Targeted:**
  - AI-powered decision support platform.
  - Functional software prototype (React + FastAPI + PostgreSQL).
  - Scopus-indexed Conference / Journal publication manuscript (Future scope for Project II).

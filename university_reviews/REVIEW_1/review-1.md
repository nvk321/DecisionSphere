## 1. Finalized Review 1 Content

### 1.1 Project Title (FINAL & LOCKED)
**DecisionSphere: A Scenario-Based Strategic Planning and Risk Evaluation Platform**

### 1.2 Aim
To design and develop an AI-powered strategic decision support platform that enables organizations to simulate business scenarios, evaluate cross-domain risks across demand forecasting, supply chain, and workforce management, and generate explainable recommendations for proactive strategic decision-making.

### 1.3 Abstract
Modern organizations operate in highly interconnected business environments where disruptions in demand, supply chain operations, and workforce availability significantly influence organizational performance. Existing decision support systems primarily analyse these domains independently, limiting their capability to evaluate cascading business impacts and future strategic scenarios. This project proposes DecisionSphere, an AI-powered strategic planning and risk evaluation platform that integrates demand forecasting, supply chain risk analysis, workforce analytics, and business impact assessment within a unified scenario simulation framework. Using real-world retail, supply chain, and HR datasets, the platform enables organizations to construct hypothetical "what-if" scenarios, estimate operational risks, evaluate business impact, and receive explainable AI-driven recommendations before implementing strategic decisions. The proposed framework aims to provide proactive, transparent, and data-driven decision support through an interactive dashboard, thereby improving organizational resilience and supporting informed business planning.

## 3. Research Gap Identified
1. Existing decision support systems analyse demand forecasting, supply chain, and HR independently in organizational silos.
2. Current industrial solutions primarily rely on retrospective historical data and static analytical models.
3. Limited framework support exists for cross-domain hypothetical "what-if" scenario simulation and business impact evaluation.
4. Existing Explainable AI methods explain isolated model predictions but do not synthesize these explanations into actionable strategic recommendations.

---

## 4. Finalized Objectives
1. Design a unified AI-powered strategic decision support platform.
2. Develop a demand forecasting module using retail sales datasets.
3. Implement supply chain risk assessment using the DataCo Smart Supply Chain dataset.
4. Develop an HR workforce risk prediction module using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.
5. Integrate cross-domain scenario simulation across demand, supply chain, and HR.
6. Assess business impact using integrated predictive analytics and transparent formulas.
7. Generate explainable AI-based strategic recommendations.
8. Visualize organizational insights through interactive dashboards and reports.
